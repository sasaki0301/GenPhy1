Kinematics: Motion Along a Straight Line

Learning Objectives

  • Define motion, position, displacement, and distance.
  • Distinguish between speed and velocity.
  • Calculate average velocity.
  • Understand acceleration and its role in motion.

1. What is Motion?

Motion occurs when an object changes its position relative to a reference point over time.

Key Idea: Motion = change in position over time.

Examples of motion include:

  • A car moving along a road
  • A ball falling from a height
  • A person walking in a straight path

2. Position, Distance, and Displacement

Position

Position describes the location of an object relative to a reference point.

Distance

Distance is the total path traveled by an object.

Displacement

Displacement is the straight-line change in position from the starting point to the ending point.

Distance vs Displacement:
  • Distance = total path (scalar)
  • Displacement = shortest path + direction (vector)

Example:

A person walks 5 m east then 3 m west.

  • Distance = 8 m
  • Displacement = 2 m east

3. Speed and Velocity

Speed

Speed is how fast an object moves.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Velocity

Velocity is speed with direction.

Velocity = Displacement ÷ Time
Key Idea:
  • Speed is scalar
  • Velocity is vector

Example:

A car travels 100 m east in 10 s.

Speed = 100 ÷ 10 = 10 m/s Velocity = 10 m/s east

4. Average Velocity

Average velocity considers total displacement over total time.

Average Velocity = Total Displacement ÷ Total Time

Example:

A student walks 10 m east in 5 s, then walks back 10 m west in 5 s.

Displacement = 0 m Time = 10 s

Average velocity = 0 ÷ 10 = 0 m/s

Even though the student moved, the displacement is zero.

5. Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

Acceleration = Change in Velocity ÷ Time
Important: Acceleration can mean:
  • Speeding up
  • Slowing down
  • Changing direction

Example:

A car increases its velocity from 5 m/s to 15 m/s in 5 seconds.

Acceleration = (15 − 5) ÷ 5 = 2 m/s²

Concept Check

Is distance a scalar or vector?

Distance is a scalar quantity.

Why can average velocity be zero even if motion occurs?

Because displacement can be zero if the object returns to its starting point.

Practice Drills

A runner travels 200 m in 20 s. Find the speed.

Speed = 200 ÷ 20 = 10 m/s

A car moves 60 m north in 6 s. Find the velocity.

Velocity = 60 ÷ 6 = 10 m/s north

A bicycle increases its velocity from 4 m/s to 12 m/s in 4 s. Find acceleration.

Acceleration = (12 − 4) ÷ 4 = 2 m/s²
